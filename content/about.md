## Hi, I'm **Shinjae Kang**

I'm a **programmer and software architect** based in **Indonesia**, working with **Modoo Digisol Indonesia** and building software across web, backend, desktop, mobile, and operations.

My work sits at the intersection of practical product engineering and infrastructure: **Laravel, VueJS, Tailwind CSS, PHP, DevOps, .NET, C#, WPF, VB, HTML, CSS, WordPress, and Flutter**. I care less about framework hype and more about systems that survive real users, real teams, and real maintenance.

I've lived and worked in Jakarta for more than 15 years, which gives this site its core point of view: a Korean senior developer operating inside the Southeast Asian tech scene, connecting ideas, tools, and business context across both worlds.

### What this site is

- A long-running personal blog about AI, software development, dev tooling, and operations
- An English-first publication with Korean and Bahasa Indonesia mirrors
- A working lab for the [zemnanet design system](/design-system/) and the zemnanet content pipeline
- A practical record of what I am testing, adopting, rejecting, and rebuilding

### What I usually write about

- AI agents and automation that need real verification, not just green dashboards
- Laravel, Vue, Tailwind, PHP, and the everyday stack behind maintainable web products
- DevOps, reliability, cron jobs, artifact checks, and production-adjacent workflows
- The Korea to Southeast Asia developer bridge: tools, markets, teams, and local constraints
- How senior developers should evaluate new tools without getting trapped by hype

### What this site is not

- A company blog. The opinions here are mine.
- A paid newsletter. No sponsors, no ads, no affiliate links.
- A posting-everything firehose. Quality gates run on every post before it ships.
