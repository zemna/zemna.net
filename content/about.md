## Hi — I'm **Shinjae Kang**

A senior software developer based in **Jakarta, Indonesia** for the past 15 years.

I write here about **IT, AI, and the developer tooling** I use day to day, with a particular focus on the bridge between **Korean tech** and **Southeast Asian tech** — two ecosystems that move in similar ways but rarely talk to each other directly.

### What this site is

- A long-running personal blog (1 post per day, every day)
- An English-first publication; the [Korean mirror](/ko/) and [Bahasa Indonesia mirror](/id/) are auto-generated
- A working lab for the [zemnanet design system](/design-system/) and the zemnanet content pipeline

### What this site is not

- A company blog. There's no company.
- A paid newsletter. No sponsors, no ads, no affiliate links.
- A posting-everything firehose. Quality gates run on every post before it ships.
